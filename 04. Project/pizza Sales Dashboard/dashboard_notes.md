# Pizza Sales Dashboard

## Business Objective

Analyse pizza sales data to understand revenue performance, customer ordering patterns, and product performance.

## Key Questions

- What is total revenue?
- How many pizzas were sold?
- Which pizzas generate the most revenue?
- Which category performs best?
- What are the busiest sales hours?
- Which month has the highest sales?

## KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1: Executive Overview

- Revenue KPIs
- Sales trend
- Category performance

### Page 2: Product Analysis

- Top-selling pizzas
- Revenue by pizza
- Revenue by size

### Page 3: Customer Ordering Behaviour

- Sales by hour
- Sales by day
- Monthly trends
