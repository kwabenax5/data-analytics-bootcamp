## Dashboard Overview

This project combines SQL analysis and Power BI visualization to evaluate pizza sales performance, identify revenue drivers, and highlight customer ordering patterns.

### Key KPIs

- **Total Revenue:** $817.86K
- **Total Orders:** 21,350
- **Average Order Value:** $38.31
- **Total Pizzas Sold:** 49,574

### Key Findings

- **July** generated the highest monthly revenue at **$72,557.90**.
- The **Classic** category generated the highest revenue at approximately **$220,053**.
- **The Thai Chicken Pizza** was the highest revenue-generating pizza.
- The top 5 pizzas by revenue were:
  1. The Thai Chicken Pizza
  2. The Barbecue Chicken Pizza
  3. The California Chicken Pizza
  4. The Classic Deluxe Pizza
  5. The Spicy Italian Pizza
- **Large pizzas** were the most popular size, accounting for **38.24%** of pizzas sold.
- The top 5 pizzas contributed **24.53%** of total revenue.
- Approximately **22 pizzas** were needed to account for 80% of cumulative revenue, suggesting revenue is spread across a broad range of products.

### Business Recommendations

- Maintain strong inventory levels for top-performing pizzas, especially The Thai Chicken Pizza.
- Prioritize the Classic category in promotions because it contributes the highest category revenue.
- Use the strong preference for Large pizzas to guide inventory planning and promotional bundles.
- Plan staffing and operational capacity around peak sales periods, especially during high-performing months.
- Continue monitoring product concentration because revenue is relatively diversified rather than dependent on only a few pizzas.
